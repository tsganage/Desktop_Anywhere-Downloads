# Mac Anywhere Support (download)

A small helper for macOS that lets someone you trust see and control your Mac for a while. You open it, give them the code it shows, and close it when you are done. It installs nothing and keeps nothing on your Mac.

**Download:** [MacAnywhereSupport-mac.dmg](https://github.com/tsganage/desktop_anywhere-downloads/releases/latest/download/MacAnywhereSupport-mac.dmg) (macOS 12.3 or later, Apple Silicon and Intel)

## How to open it

1. Open the downloaded `.dmg`, then double-click **Mac Anywhere Support**.
2. macOS will say it cannot check the app for malware, because it is not notarized by Apple. Click **OK**, then:
   - **macOS 12, 13 and 14:** right-click (or Control-click) **Mac Anywhere Support**, choose **Open**, then click **Open**. If there is no Open button, open **System Settings, Security & Privacy** (Privacy & Security on 13 and 14), General, and click **Open Anyway** next to its name.
   - **macOS 15 and later:** open **System Settings, Privacy & Security**, scroll down, and click **Open Anyway** next to its name. Confirm.
3. Allow the two permissions it asks for (showing your screen, and controlling the mouse and keyboard). Click **Reopen the helper** if it asks.
4. Give the person the code and password it shows. When they connect, it asks you to **Allow** them.

Closing the helper ends everything. You can eject the disk image afterwards.

One download works on every supported Mac. On macOS 12 it shares the screen and lets them control it, but without sound; macOS 13 and later get everything.

## Only use it with someone you trust

While the helper is open and you have pressed **Allow**, the other person can do anything on your Mac that you can. If someone you do not know asked you to download this, do not.

## What it does not do

It never gives access to your files, clipboard or printers, never locks or unlocks your Mac, and never blacks out your screen. It cannot be used unless you read out its code and password and then allow the connection.
